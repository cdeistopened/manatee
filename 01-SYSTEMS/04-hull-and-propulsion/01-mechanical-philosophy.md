# 01 — Mechanical Philosophy: Extreme Reduction

*Aerospace discipline applied to appropriate-technology hardware: remove every part that can fail, standardize around hardware-store supply chains, and design for local repair.*

---

## 1. The Core Rule: "Remove Everything"

In open-water robotic engineering, moving parts exposed to water, sunlight, and biological growth fail rapidly:
- Mechanical rudders foul with pond weeds within hours.
- Submerged propeller shafts wrap with filamentous algae and burn out motor seals.
- Complex electronic steering linkages seize from corrosion.

The Manatee design applies strict aerospace reduction discipline:
1. **No underwater propellers**: Water is moved entirely through smooth internal pipe passages.
2. **No mechanical steering rudders**: Steering is accomplished via differential jet thrust or natural wind vane drifting.
3. **No custom molded fiberglass tooling**: Every structural member is standard off-the-shelf stock cut with a hand saw.

---

## 2. The Hardware-Store Supply Chain

Every primary component must be purchasable at a local plumbing and agricultural supply store anywhere in the world:

| System Component | Material Selection | Sourcing Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Pontoons** | Schedule 40 PVC Pipe (4" or 6" diameter) with glued caps | 100% waterproof, UV resistant, crush resistant, ~$25 per length |
| **Chassis Rails** | 6061-T6 Aluminum angle or galvanized Unistrut channel | Structural rigidity, corrosion resistant, slotted for easy adjustment |
| **Fasteners** | 316 Stainless Steel bolts, U-bolts, jaw-and-jaw turnbuckles | Zero rust in wet environment, standard imperial/metric threading |
| **Collection Vessel** | Translucent HDPE 5-gallon pail or 55-gallon drum | Chemical resistant, translucent for visual liquid level, universal standard |

---

## 3. Maintenance Simplicity

The entire machine can be disassembled and serviced with three basic tools:
- A 1/2" socket wrench / box wrench.
- A flathead screwdriver.
- A hand hacksaw.
