# Cybernetics: Feedback Loops & the Living Machine

*Due-diligence dossier — Mobula / EVIE-2c (Rudy Behrens, Barsoom Engineering & Advanced Research / Solaris Cybernetics). Prepared 2026-06-17.*

## What it is (plain language)

The Mobula is pitched as a self-running ocean robot that is supposed to behave less like a drone and more like an organism. The story is a single big loop: the robot steers toward sunlight (phototaxis), the sunlight grows algae in an onboard greenhouse, the algae are crushed into crude diesel-like fuel by a cavitation reactor, some of that fuel/energy runs the robot, and the robot uses that energy to find more light and grow more algae. Waste CO2 from the engine and decomposition is fed back into the algae tanks; nutrient water from the refinery is recycled back into the greenhouse. On paper, it eats sunlight and CO2, excretes paraffin, and tends itself.

The intellectual lineage is explicit and, unusually for a fringe inventor, correctly attributed. Behrens is not freelancing a metaphor — one of the source files (`9rules[1].txt`) is a **verbatim copy of John Todd's published "Nine Principles for Designing Living Machines"** (microbial diversity, linked ecosystems, pulsed exchanges, self-design and self-repair). The wider manifesto, Virgo City (`vc310B.txt`), names R. Buckminster Fuller (synergy, tensegrity, "we are all millionaires") and John Todd directly as the design ancestors, and builds everything around "tetraponics" — a four-element (air/light/water/minerals) closed ecological loop with deliberate *redundancy*: two symbiotic cycles (fish↔plant and plant↔plant/bacteria) running in parallel so that if one is disrupted the other holds the system near equilibrium. That is a genuine cybernetic instinct: design for homeostasis, not just function.

So the honest framing is: **the ambition is a homeostatic, self-regulating "living technology" in the Todd/Beer/Fuller tradition. The artifact, as documented, is a loosely-coupled chain of subsystems with one real closed-loop controller (phototaxis) and a long list of loops that are asserted but not built.** It is a living-machine *aspiration* wrapped around a Braitenberg light-seeker.

## How it works (the real mechanism, with numbers)

**The one real control loop — phototaxis.** The "AI" (`AIsourcecode1.txt`, MASTER_CONTROL_AI.md "Prospero") is a ~40-line LOGO/Cricket program. Six light sensors (a–f) are each sampled 3× and averaged (`avgread`); whichever sensor reads brightest relative to the mean of the other five sets a `final` direction code 1–6; the two thrusters then steer that way (`a, thisway` / `b, thatway`) until the bright sensor changes. If the global average is below 20, it idles (`setfinal 0`) and re-checks after a 6–9 second wait. This is a textbook **Braitenberg vehicle 2/3 (reactive phototaxis)** — a proportional-ish steering reflex, no map, no state, no learning. It is a closed negative-feedback loop (error = light asymmetry → corrective steering → reduced asymmetry) and it genuinely works as a homeostat in Ashby's sense: it drives the vehicle to a stable attractor (maximum light). Note a bug worth flagging: the averages use LOGO's left-to-right precedence with no parentheses, so `a + b + c + d + e + f / 6` actually computes `a+b+c+d+e+(f/6)` — the "average" is wrong. The reflex still roughly works because the comparison is monotonic, but the threshold logic is not what the author thinks it is.

**The asserted loops (the "living machine" claims).** These come from the subsystem docs and process files:
- **Growth loop:** sealed Lexan greenhouse, LED photoperiod ≥14h, CO2 bubblers fed from engine exhaust + atmosphere, 75–85°F. Claimed algae doubling time **90–120 min** and yield **5.62 lb biomass/ft³/day** of an "S3 blend" (spirulina, azolla, *Gracilaria*, *Lemna*, *Wolffia* — `S3algaeRecipe.txt`).
- **Harvest loop:** a light sensor watches biomass concentration; when it exceeds ~6% by weight the dense fraction is pumped to the processor (`9x9HVO_process.txt`). Micro-bubbler/protein-skimmer dewatering to ~30% moisture (BIOMASS_PROCESSING.md).
- **Conversion loop:** 300 psi pump → cavitating LVKN nozzle → ~3% of each pass converted to hydrocarbons via hydrous pyrolysis; converted fuel floats and is skimmed, unconverted mass recirculates (`LVKNprocess.txt`, `9x9HVO_process.txt`).
- **Energy/propulsion loop:** wind turbine + 45W PV + a Seebeck/thermoelectric generator on the 630°F vortex heat → battery → MinnKota thrusters + hydraulic jets; plus a passive "whale tail" wave-thrust vane (POWER_AND_PROPULSION.md).
- **Material recycling loop:** refinery nutrient water + mineral ash returned to greenhouse; a side tank decomposes biomass to generate CO2 (ENVIRONMENTAL_CONTROL.md, `9x9HVO_process.txt`). Claimed "100%" recycle and "active carbon sink."

**The energy ledger Behrens claims** (`LVKNprocess.txt`): accelerating 1 gal to 100 ft/s ≈ 204.84 BTU; at 3% conversion/pass that is 6,828 BTU spent to convert a gallon's worth; output ≈127,000 BTU/gal; therefore conversion cost = **5.34% of energy produced**. The cavitation core is claimed at 15,000 rpm, 1500°F, "absolute zero" pressure in a 1000-Å zone. These numbers are the load-bearing wall of the whole cybernetic story — if the energy balance is wrong, the central loop is open, not closed, and the machine is not autonomous.

## New-vs-old (what the 2024 PRIME / EVIE-2c design changed for THIS system)

The two 2024 SketchUp files (`VBShdl-1m5manatee2b1PRIME.skp`, dated Aug 2024, and the `+BimodalTurbine` variant, Jul 2024) are the first design change in ~8 years (prior `.skp` work clusters in 2012–2016). From the preview PNGs:

- **Form factor flip.** Old Mobula renders (06_Media `BEAR Mobula-1 M10/M20a/M40`) were biomorphic manta-ray hulls. PRIME/EVIE-2c is an **explicit tensegrity frame** (the red struts read as isolated tension/compression members in the Fuller sense) slung between **twin blue pontoons**, with a central gray **growth/processor drum** amidships. This is the first time the vehicle visually *is* the Fuller tensegrity it always claimed in the manifesto — the cybernetic/Fuller framing moved from text into geometry.
- **Amphibious wheels at the pontoon ends.** Both previews show wheels at the four pontoon tips. This is a new *capability* loop, not just a hull change: an amphibious robot can self-recover, self-launch, and self-dock without the HUB station's robotic arm — i.e., it closes part of the "fault tolerance / return-to-port" loop that the old design pushed onto external infrastructure (HUB_SYSTEM.md, OPERATIONS_CONTROL_CENTER.md).
- **BimodalTurbine variant** adds a **horizontal-axis turbine on a central mast** (the older docs specified a vertical-axis "bird-safe" turbine). "Bimodal" implies the turbine is meant to harvest both wind (above water) and possibly current (below) — a second energy-input loop. This *strengthens* the energy side of the cybernetic balance on paper but is unmodeled.
- **What did NOT change:** the controller. The "A.I" folder still ships the same 2012 `AIsourcecode1.doc`; the 2019 v2 control board (`Circuits/`) and `LowellAI.png` are a hardware refresh, not a new control architecture. **The 2024 redesign upgraded the body and the energy harvest but left the brain a 2012 reactive phototaxis reflex.** For a "living machine" claim, that is the wrong end to leave frozen.

## Evidence & maturity (documented / built / tested vs asserted)

**Documented + independently tested (the strongest evidence in the whole corpus):**
- The **AECOM / PACE Labs report** (`Lab-Results/SC1aecom1report2.md`, `BEARfuelTest1.pdf`) is real third-party lab work. A robot's reactor was bench-reconfigured and treated 5-gal Florida algae samples using **37 Wh** of electricity, no chemicals, producing **paraffin** (visible on the surface) plus residual biomass, with measured **reductions in nitrogen and phosphorus** in the water column. This validates *that the cavitation reactor makes paraffin and remediates nutrients* — the chemistry end of the conversion loop is real, at 5-gallon batch scale.
- Field operation is documented (text claims) in Port Norris and the Meadowlands, NJ (estuarine, variable salinity), plus the 2015 farm-pond prototype and 2023 marina test (photos in `04_Prototype-Testing/`).

**Built but not instrumented:** the phototaxis controller (code exists, Cricket/Super Cricket BOM exists, frame tests photographed). No telemetry, no logged closed-loop runs.

**Asserted only (no data in corpus):**
- The **energy balance** (5.34% figure, the 100 ft/s / 3%-per-pass / 127,000 BTU chain). The AECOM test measured electricity *in* (37 Wh) but the report does not state fuel-energy *out* per Wh, so it does not close the EROI loop.
- Algae **90–120 min doubling** and **5.62 lb/ft³/day** yield.
- "**100% recycle**," "**active carbon sink**," closed CO2 loop.
- "Redundant marine-hardened processors," "homeostatic control," "Drift & Correct," "failure resilience" (MASTER_CONTROL_AI.md) — these are described as functions but the only code present is the single phototaxis file. There is **no homeostatic controller in the source** for thermal, chemical, pH, harvest-timing, or fault states. Those loops exist as prose, not as logic.

## Validation status (current science / engineering)

- **Living-machine lineage is real and credible.** John Todd's Eco-Machines are a proven, operating technology: the South Burlington, VT Eco-Machine treats ~80,000 gal/day of municipal wastewater (EPA Environmental Merit Award); Oberlin's Lewis Center recycles ~70% of building water; systems self-design by importing organisms from surrounding ecosystems. The Nine Principles Behrens reproduces are Todd's actual design canon. **PLAUSIBLE / well-founded** that a linked, diverse, pulsed ecological system can self-regulate and self-repair. *Caveat:* Todd's machines are large, stationary, open to their environment, and operate on **ecological time** (the self-design Todd praises took 25 years to mature). Behrens wants the same emergent homeostasis inside a small, sealed, mobile, weeks-long-mission box. The thing that makes a Living Machine "alive" — slow ecological succession and outside colonization — is exactly what a sealed shipboard reactor on a fast mission **cannot** have. The lineage is borrowed correctly but applied to a regime where its core mechanism is suppressed. **PLAUSIBLE lineage, DUBIOUS transfer.**
- **Phototaxis as a homeostat: PLAUSIBLE.** Braitenberg/Ashby-style reactive control is real, robust, and exactly the right tool for light-seeking. This is the one subsystem that genuinely is cybernetic and genuinely works. (Minus the averaging-precedence bug.)
- **Algae doubling 90–120 min: DUBIOUS→FALSE.** Peer-reviewed photobioreactor literature puts best-case doubling near **~24 hours** and productivity at **1–5 g/L/day** (often <1 g/L/day outdoors with day/night cycles). A 90–120 min doubling is **~12–16× faster than the literature's best case** and is not physically supported for the listed species at sea. The yield (5.62 lb/ft³/day ≈ 90 g/L/day) is roughly **20–90× above** demonstrated productivity.
- **Energy balance (5.34%): DUBIOUS, unproven.** The Euler/Boyle arithmetic is internally cute but rests on three unverified assumptions — that exactly 3% converts per pass, that the product is 127,000 BTU/gal, and that the cavitation process is near-adiabatic (the report claims "thermodynamically reversible," which sonochemistry is decidedly **not** — cavitation collapse is strongly dissipative). Independent hydrodynamic-cavitation literature treats it as an energy-*intensive* process; net-energy-positive fuel production via cavitation is an extraordinary claim with no closed measurement in this corpus.
- **"Absolute zero pressure," 1500°F, 15,000 rpm in a 1000-Å zone: FALSE as stated.** "Absolute zero pressure" is a perfect vacuum, which a transient cavitation void does not reach; sonoluminescence hotspots reach thousands of K for nanoseconds but the steady-state "1500°F core spinning at 15,000 rpm" description is not how cavitation physics is characterized. The chemistry output (paraffin) is real (AECOM); the **mechanistic narrative explaining it is not validated and is partly nonphysical**.
- **Stafford Beer / VSM lens: the system is NOT yet viable.** Beer's Viable System Model says an autonomous system needs five functions: S1 operations, S2 coordination (anti-oscillation between subsystems), S3 control/audit, S4 intelligence (sense the environment, plan), S5 policy/identity. The Mobula has **S1** (the subsystems) and a thin **S4-ish reflex** (phototaxis = environmental sensing → action). It has **essentially no S2 or S3**: nothing coordinates the growth, harvest, conversion, and propulsion loops against each other or arbitrates conflicts (e.g., steer toward light vs. hold position to finish a harvest batch). By Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety, the controller's variety (a 6-state light reflex) is **far below** the variety of the environment it must survive (storms, fouling, salinity swings, contamination, battery state, hull faults). **A homeostat for *heading* is not a homeostat for the *organism*.** On the VSM test, this is a viable navigation reflex bolted to a non-viable whole.

## Red flags & open questions

1. **The brain didn't evolve with the body.** Eight years of CAD iteration (2016→2024 PRIME) and the control code is still the 2012 phototaxis file. A "3rd Generation Prospero AI" with "homeostatic control" and "failure resilience" is claimed in MASTER_CONTROL_AI.md but **no such code exists in the corpus** — only the light-seeker.
2. **Most "loops" are open.** CO2 feedback, harvest timing, thermal/pH regulation, contamination response, fault recovery, and energy-budget management are described in prose but have no controller. A living machine is defined by closed regulatory loops; these are aspirational.
3. **The headline physics numbers are unfalsifiable as presented** and partly nonphysical ("absolute zero pressure," "thermodynamically reversible cavitation"). The one hard third-party datum (37 Wh treats 5 gal, makes paraffin) is real but does **not** close the energy-positive claim.
4. **Biology is 1–2 orders of magnitude optimistic** (doubling time, yield). The whole self-fueling premise depends on growth rates the literature does not support at sea.
5. **Self-design is structurally impossible in a sealed, mobile, short-mission vessel** — the exact feature that makes Todd's Eco-Machines self-regulate (slow open-ended ecological succession) is engineered out here.
6. **Averaging bug** in the only working controller (operator precedence) — minor, but it means the threshold/idle logic doesn't do what the author believes.
7. **Open question:** does the BimodalTurbine variant exist to fix a known energy deficit (i.e., did the inventor discover the loop doesn't close and add more harvest)? Worth asking Behrens directly.

## Concrete improvements (buildable upgrades + cheap de-risking experiments)

**Cheap experiments that de-risk the cybernetic claims (do these first):**
- **Close the energy loop on the bench.** Re-run the AECOM-style 5-gal test but instrument it: log Wh in *and* measured BTU/gal out (bomb calorimeter on the paraffin) over many passes. One clean number — net BTU out per Wh in — either kills or proves the 5.34% claim. ~$2–5k of lab time.
- **Measure real doubling time** of the actual S3 blend under the actual LED/CO2 regime in a single tank. A 2-week jar study settles the 90-min vs 24-hr question for ~$500.
- **Log one phototaxis run** with a cheap datalogger (sensor values + heading) to confirm the homeostat converges and to expose the averaging bug in the wild.

**Buildable control-system upgrades (turn subsystems into a viable system):**
- **Add the missing S2/S3 supervisory layer.** A small state machine (even on an ESP32/RPi) that arbitrates modes — `SEEK_LIGHT`, `HOLD_AND_HARVEST`, `PROCESS`, `LOW_POWER`, `FAULT/RETURN` — with explicit transitions on battery %, biomass density, fault flags. This is the single highest-leverage change: it converts a reflex into a Beer-viable controller. Keep the LOGO phototaxis as the S1 heading reflex underneath.
- **Close the CO2 loop with a sensor.** Dissolved-CO2 / pH probe in the growth tank driving the bubbler valve = a real homeostatic loop instead of an asserted one. Cheap, high credibility.
- **Harvest-timing as closed loop, not threshold.** The 6%-density light sensor exists; make it drive a PID-ish controller that also accounts for fuel-bladder level and battery state (don't start an energy-hungry process run on a low battery).
- **Fault tolerance via watchdog + amphibious self-recovery.** The 2024 wheels make "drive ashore on fault" a real recovery loop — wire it to the fault state above.
- **Fix the averaging precedence bug** (parenthesize the means) so the idle/threshold logic is correct.

## Kit / license angle

There is a genuinely sellable, low-controversy product hiding inside the over-claimed whole: **the cybernetic light-seeking living-machine STEM/research kit.** Two distinct offers:

1. **"Braitenberg-on-water" educational kit / open-source controller.** The phototaxis loop is real, charming, and pedagogically perfect — it's the cleanest physical demonstration of cybernetic feedback, Ashby's homeostat, and Braitenberg vehicles you can buy. Package the LOGO/Cricket (or a modern Arduino/ESP32 port we write) + the 6-sensor steering reflex + a small twin-thruster pontoon as a **tabletop/pond living-machine kit** for schools and makers. This squarely serves the Virgo City manifesto's *own* stated goal — "schools can afford biospheres of their own, to teach children ecology in a Hands-on way." It sells the *idea* (self-regulating living technology) without selling the contested physics. Add the (fixed) supervisory state machine as the "level 2" build.

2. **License the IP via the existing template.** The `2022PatentLicenseEditableGENERAL` package + US Patent #7,750,494 + the 3%-of-gross royalty terms (`Study_Plans_Instructions.txt`) are already drafted. The *defensible, demonstrated* asset to license is **the cavitation/hydrous-pyrolysis reactor as an algae-bloom remediation + paraffin-recovery unit** (AECOM-validated: removes N/P, makes saleable paraffin, 37 Wh/5 gal, no chemicals). That is a real environmental-remediation claim with third-party data behind it — far easier to sell than "self-fueling autonomous ocean fleet." Sell the remediation reactor on its proven merits; license the autonomy/living-machine framing as the aspirational research platform on top.

The throughline for skeptical buyers: **buy the loop that's closed (phototaxis reflex; bench-proven reactor chemistry), fund the loops that aren't (energy balance, biology, supervisory control) as instrumented experiments — don't buy the self-fueling fleet until the energy ledger is measured, not asserted.**
