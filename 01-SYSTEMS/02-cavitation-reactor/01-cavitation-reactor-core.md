# 01 — Hydrodynamic Cavitation Reactor Core

*The physical science of chemical-free cell lysis and lipid extraction via localized sonic cavitation shockwaves.*

## 1. Principle of Hydrodynamic Cavitation

Hydrodynamic cavitation occurs when a pressurized liquid passes through a sharp constriction (an orifice or venturi nozzle), causing local fluid velocity to surge and static pressure to drop below the vapor pressure of water ($P_{\text{local}} < P_{\text{vapor}}$). Microscopic vapor bubbles form instantly, and as the fluid expands into the downstream chamber, local pressure recovers, causing violent bubble collapse:

```
High-Pressure Fluid (200–400 psi)
                 │
                 ▼
     ┌───────────────────────┐
     │ CONSTRICTION ORIFICE  │  Velocity Surges (v > 30 m/s)
     │ (Ceramic Insert)      │  Pressure Plummets (P < P_vapor)
     └───────────┬───────────┘  Micro-Vapor Bubbles Form
                 │
                 ▼
     ┌───────────────────────┐
     │ EXPANSION CHAMBER     │  Pressure Recovers Instantly
     │ (Violent Collapse)    │  Bubbles Implode Asymmetrically
     └───────────┬───────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
Localized Shockwaves & Micro-Jets:
• Local Temperature: > 3,000–5,000 K (in micro-zone)
• Local Pressure Shock: > 1,000–5,000 atm
• Extreme Shear Rate: > 10^7 s^-1
                 │
                 ▼
Mechanical Lysis of Microalgae Cell Walls:
• Intracellular lipid droplets and hydrocarbon wax released
• Bulk fluid temperature rises by less than 1°C
```

## 2. Why Cavitation Outperforms Chemical Extraction

Traditional solvent extraction uses hexane or chloroform to dissolve cell lipids, requiring hazardous solvent recovery stills and creating toxic waste. Thermal pyrolysis heats the entire bulk fluid to 350°C, requiring massive fossil fuel or electrical heat input.

Hydrodynamic cavitation achieves **localized micro-pyrolysis conditions** purely through hydraulic shear:
1. **Chemical-Free**: Uses only the kinetic energy of water.
2. **Cold Bulk Fluid**: 99.9% of the water remains at ambient pond temperature ($20–25^\circ\text{C}$), saving the massive enthalpy of heating water ($4.184 \text{ J/g}\cdot^\circ\text{C}$).
3. **High Cell Lysis Ratio**: >85% cell wall disruption in a single pass at 250–350 psi.
