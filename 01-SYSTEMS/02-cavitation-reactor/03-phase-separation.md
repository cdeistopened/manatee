# 03 — Phase Separation & Lipid Wax Recovery

*Gravity stratification, flotation foam skimmers, and post-cavitation hydrocarbon harvesting.*

---

## 1. Post-Cavitation Fluid Composition

Effluent emerging from the cavitation nozzle is a tri-phasic mixture:
1. **Clarified Aqueous Phase**: Depleted water stripped of cellular biomass ($90–95\%$ by volume).
2. **Dense Hydrophobic Lipid/Wax Layer**: Released hydrocarbons, waxes, and fatty acid methyl lipids ($\rho \approx 0.88–0.92 \text{ g/cm}^3$).
3. **Insoluble Cellular Debris (Cake)**: Ruptured protein cell walls and duckweed fiber ($\rho \approx 1.05–1.15 \text{ g/cm}^3$).

---

## 2. Gravity Separation & Flotation Skimming

Because the liberated lipids are non-polar and have a specific gravity lower than water ($\text{SG} \approx 0.884$, confirmed by Intertek ASTM D4052 assay at 28.5° API), they spontaneously rise to the surface in a calm collection chamber:

```
Cavitation Effluent Inflow

             ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ STRATIFICATION CHAMBER (Translucent Collection Vessel)  │
│                                                        │
│   ▲  [ RECOVERED WAX / LIPID LAYER ] (Top Phase)       │
│   │  Dense Hydrocarbon Cake (37–46 MJ/kg)              │
│   │  Skimmed off top valve into storage jar            │
│   ───┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────│
│      │                                                 │

│      │  Continuous underflow discharge overboard       │
│   ───┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────│
│   ▼  │                                                 │
│      ▼  [ RESIDUAL CELL PROTEIN MUD ] (Bottom Phase)   │
│         Drained periodically for garden fertilizer     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 3. Storage and Solidification

At ambient pond temperatures ($< 22^\circ\text{C}$ / $72^\circ\text{F}$), the high-molecular-weight lipid fraction solidifies into a soft paraffin wax. This wax is:
- **100% Water-Insoluble**: Floats cleanly atop the water column.
- **Easy to Scoop**: Requires no liquid pumps to harvest — simply lift the solidified wax cake with a perforated paddle.
- **Stable for Years**: Does not oxidize or go rancid when kept sealed, functioning as the primary bio-battery store.
