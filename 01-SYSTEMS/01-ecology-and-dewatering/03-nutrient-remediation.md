# 03 — Nutrient Remediation & Watershed Balance

*How the Manatee turns agricultural nitrogen and phosphorus pollution into valuable dispatchable wax while restoring aquatic ecosystems.*

---

## 1. The Eutrophication Cycle

Agricultural fertilizer runoff (nitrogen and phosphorus) causes severe freshwater eutrophication:
1. Surplus nutrients trigger explosive algal blooms.
2. Algal biomass dies and sinks to the benthic zone.
3. Aerobic bacteria consume all dissolved oxygen to decompose the dead algae, creating anoxic dead zones (fish kills, toxic cyanobacteria blooms).
4. Phosphorus is released back into the water column from anoxic sediments, perpetuating the cycle.

---

## 2. The Remediation Loop: Nutrient Export as Value

The Manatee breaks the eutrophication cycle by **physically exporting organic biomass ashore**, rather than letting it decompose in place:

```
Agricultural Runoff (N & P Inflow)
              │
              ▼
    [ Photosynthetic Growth ]
    Sunlight fixes dissolved N & P into living cell tissue
              │
              ▼
    [ Surface Harvesting ]
    Manatee skims biomass continuously
              │
              ▼
    [ Hydrodynamic Cavitation & Separation ]
    Hydrocarbon wax recovered  +  Nutrient-rich protein cake collected
              │                                      │
              ▼                                      ▼
    Dispatchable Energy Wax             Organic Soil Fertilizer / Bio-Char
    (37–46 MJ/kg store)                 (Returned to agricultural fields)
```

---

## 3. Remediation Economics

In conventional water treatment, clearing a 5-acre agricultural pond of seasonal cyanobacteria costs **$4,000 to $12,000 annually** in copper sulfate and algaecide applications — which poison the aquatic food web and leave dead biomass to rot on the pond bottom.

The Manatee delivers continuous chemical-free water remediation:
- **Zero chemical cost**: Clears the water purely through mechanical skimming and cavitation lysis.
- **Permanent nutrient removal**: Every kilogram of harvested biomass exports ~80g nitrogen and ~10g phosphorus from the watershed.
- **Dual Revenue**: Remediation savings pay for the machine, while the recovered wax provides free off-grid energy.
