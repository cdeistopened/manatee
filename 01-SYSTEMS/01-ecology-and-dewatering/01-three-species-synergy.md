# 01 — The Three-Species Polyculture Engine

*The biological core of the Manatee: how a synergistic polyculture of microalgae, duckweed (*Lemna*), and beneficial microbial biofilms solves the historic bottleneck of algal energy.*

## 1. The Polyculture Principle

Mono-culture raceway ponds and closed photobioreactors suffer from extreme vulnerability to contamination, light-saturation shock, and staggering de-watering costs. The Manatee operates on an ecological polyculture principle inspired by John Todd’s *Living Machines*:

## 2. The Three Organism Roles

### A. Microalgae (*Chlorella*, *Scenedesmus*, *Nannochloropsis*)
- **Function**: The high-rate solar nano-machine.
- **Doubling Time**: 6 to 24 hours under peak summer sunlight.
- **Biochemical Output**: Neutral triacylglycerols (TAGs), polar membrane lipids, and hydrocarbon waxes.
- **Physical Profile**: 2–10 µm spherical/elliptical single cells suspended in the water column.

### B. Duckweed (*Lemna minor*, *Spirodela polyrhiza*)
- **Function**: The macroscopic surface harvester, shade regulator, and mechanical de-watering filter.
- **Doubling Time**: 16 to 48 hours via asexual budding.
- **Biochemical Profile**: High crude protein (30–40% dry weight), low lignin, high starch.
- **Physical Role**: Forms a floating green mat with dense submerged root hairs (5–20 mm length). As water flows through the root zone, microalgae physically adhere to the root matrix, aggregating into an entangled, easily filterable mesh.

### C. Beneficial Microbial Consortium (Biofilms & Fungi)
- **Function**: Bio-flocculator and biochemical bridge.
- **Mechanism**: Bacteria excrete natural extracellular polymeric substances (EPS) that neutralize negative electrostatic charges on microalgal cell walls, triggering spontaneous bio-flocculation onto duckweed roots.
- **Nutrient Cycling**: Solubilizes agricultural phosphorus ($PO_4^{3-}$) and nitrifies ammonium ($NH_4^+$) into nitrates ($NO_3^-$), feeding the algae at maximum photosynthetic velocity.
