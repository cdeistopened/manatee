# The Manatee — Two-Kit Manifest & Price Bands

*How the machine ships. The 55-gallon reactor drum is **both** the reactor vessel **and** the shipping crate: the smart, corrosion-critical, hard-to-source "brains + hardware" pack **inside** the drum (interior ~22.5 in dia × 33 in tall), and every bulky member that can't fit — pontoons/floats, frame stock, mast, solar panel — is a **printed cut-list** the builder buys locally. Two kits are offered: a cheapest-faithful **Demonstrator** and the improved **Real-Machine** that actually works. All prices are real US retail from `bom-sourced.json` and `03-ELECTRONICS-GUIDE.md`; anything not firmly quoted is marked **approx**.*

**The packing rule:** any rigid member **> 33 in** ships local (freight on 10-ft stock exceeds the metal's value). Everything else — under 33 in, corrosion-critical, or not reliably stocked in marine grade at a hardware store — rides in the drum. The two commodity heavies that *would* fit but ship local anyway: the **lithium battery** (ground-ship/hazmat, swap-anywhere part) and the **rigid solar panel** (fragile glass, locally stocked). Aerosols (paint, some coatings) are ORM-D and also ship local.

---

## KIT 1 — The Demonstrator ("science-fair" tier)

Cheapest faithful proof-of-concept, in the **as-built spirit**: Route B round-tube (6-in PVC) pontoons, a local low-cost frame, the **simplest Tier-1 analog reflex** (dumb PWM charge control, discrete reflex board), and a small self-contained high-pressure pump standing in for the triplex. It proves the one thing that matters — *cavitation lyses algae, water leaves cleaner, a little wax renders out* — and instruments the Wh-in/oil-out question cheaply. It does **not** get the full separation train, the marine triplex, or the corrosion-hardened rigging.

## Margin rationale (why the kits are priced lean)

The distribution goal is a **numbers game — wide reach**, not per-unit profit. Three reasons to hold gross margin at ~25–35% rather than the 50–60% typical of niche hardware:

1. **Every kit sold is a telemetry node.** The defensible asset is the standardized fleet dataset (energy-balance ratio and turbidity-delta keyed to `config_id`), not the open-source hardware anyone can clone. More units in more ponds = a bigger moat. Price to maximize units.
2. **Value is captured downstream, not at the kit sale** — the Observer data network, IP licensing (only after lab tests + title search), and recurring consumables (orifice inserts, coalescer media, turbidity sensors, filter screens).
3. **The kit's real product is de-risked sourcing** — pre-marrying a Cat triplex head to a BLDC drive (no COTS 24–48 V triplex exists), marine-grade 316 rigging, and the corrosion-critical bits a builder can't reliably assemble alone. That's the honest reason to pay a margin at all; keep it modest so the price never becomes the reason someone doesn't build.
