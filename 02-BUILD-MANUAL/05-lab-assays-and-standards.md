## 2. Scientific Interpretation & Honest Claims Discipline

1. **Energy Density Exceeds Standard Biodiesel**:
   - At **$39.86 \text{ MJ/kg}$**, the sample possesses higher energy density than oxygenated fatty-acid methyl esters (FAME biodiesel, ~37 MJ/kg), indicating significant hydrocarbon / paraffin alkane content rather than pure lipid esters.
2. **The "Biodiesel" Label Firewall**:
   - The lab report designates the sample name as `"SUBMITTED BIO-DIESEL"` under `"Drawn By: Submitted Samples"`. **This is the inventor's own label, NOT a laboratory molecular finding.**
   - ASTM D4052 and D240 measure physical density and heat of combustion; they do not perform Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) molecular speciation.
3. **Standing Rule**:
   - **Always cite the measured numbers**: *"API gravity 28.5°, gross heat of combustion 17,137 BTU/lb (39.9 MJ/kg)."*
   - **Never claim**: *"This is certified ASTM D6751 pump diesel fuel."*
   - It is an energy-dense paraffin hydrocarbon wax suitable for candles, heating torches, wax lamps, and high-temperature thermal storage.
