# 03 — Commissioning, Float Balancing & Water Tuning

*Step-by-step procedure for launching the Manatee, trimming pontoon balance, and tuning cavitation nozzle operating pressures.*

---

## 1. Pre-Launch Dry Checklist
1. **Piping Pressure Test**: Soap-bubble leak check on all PVC pontoon endcaps at 3 psi air pressure.
2. **Electrical Isolation**: Verify zero continuity between 12V positive rail and aluminum chassis.
3. **Fuse Rating**: Verify Blue Sea 5175 30A fuse installed in terminal holder.
4. **Relay Cutoff Thresholds**: Calibrate analog comparator potentiometer: disconnect at $11.8\text{V}$, reconnect at $12.8\text{V}$.

---

## 2. First-Float Ballasting & Waterline Trim

```
Target Waterline: 3.25"–3.50" on 4" PVC Tubes (75% submerged)
                ┌────────────────────────────────┐
                │          DECK EQUIPMENT        │
   WATERLINE ~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~ WATERLINE
             ───┴────────────────────────────────┴───
               \________________________________/
                   PONTOON SUBMERGED DISPLACEMENT
```

1. **Place vessel gently in calm shallow water**.
2. **Check Fore-Aft Level**:
   - The rear collection bucket and pump should sit slightly lower (~0.5" deeper draft) than the front skimmer.
   - Adjust battery box position along the center unistrut channel until level trim is achieved.
3. **Check Port-Starboard Roll Balance**:
   - Align solar panel mast directly along the central axis of symmetry.
   - Tighten jaw-and-jaw turnbuckles to square the frame diagonally.

---

## 3. Hydraulic Tuning & Pressure Adjustment

1. **Prime the Intake**: Fill the intake strainer basket with pond water.
2. **Engage the Pump**: Switch manual override ON.
3. **Monitor Pressure Gauge**:
   - **Target Running Pressure**: $250 \text{ to } 350 \text{ psi}$ ($17–24 \text{ bar}$).
   - **If Pressure < 200 psi**: Check for air leaks on pump intake, or reduce orifice size to $0.028"$.
   - **If Pressure > 400 psi**: Open pressure-relief bypass regulator slightly, or inspect ceramic orifice for sediment blockage.
4. **Inspect Reaction Thruster**: Confirm smooth, continuous forward drift ($0.15–0.30 \text{ knots}$) without spinning or yaw oscillations.
